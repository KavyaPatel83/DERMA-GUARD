About these numbers. They describe the benchmark partition of PAD-UFES-20 (1,953 training, 229 validation and 459 test rows). The partition is not grouped by patient, and melanoma has only 10 test lesions, so treat them as a comparison between configurations and not as accuracy on new patients.
