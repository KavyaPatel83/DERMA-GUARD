Responsible use

Safety & limitations

DERMA-GUARD is a research prototype. It has not been clinically validated and must not be used to diagnose, treat or rule out any condition.

See a doctor promptly if a spot…

  • Asymmetric — one half does not match the other
  • Border irregular, ragged or blurred
  • Colour varies — several shades of brown, black, red or white
  • Diameter larger than about 6 mm
  • Evolving — changing in size, shape, colour, or starting to bleed, itch or crust

Known limitations

0 · Reported benchmark numbers were not held-out measurements

The original training script evaluated on rows it had trained on and adjusted probabilities to hit target accuracies. Re-evaluated on unseen rows, accuracy is about 61–65% for image-based models and about 78% for metadata-only. SCC recall is very low. See the Evidence and audit pages.

1 · Synthetic narratives

PAD-UFES-20 contains no free-text clinical notes. The text modality is generated from structured fields, so it is cleaner and more diagnosis-correlated than real patient writing. Text-inclusive results are likely optimistic.

2 · Retrospective evaluation on one dataset

All figures come from a held-out split of a single public dataset collected in Brazil. There is no prospective or multi-centre validation, and skin types 5–6 are almost absent.

3 · Class imbalance

Melanoma is about 2% of cases. A handful of test examples makes its metrics unstable; high aggregate accuracy can hide poor performance on rare classes.

4 · Acquisition is advisory

When the system says “acquire more evidence” it only recommends — nothing is collected automatically, and the cost model is hand-defined.

5 · Heuristic thresholds

Consensus and sufficiency cut-offs are tuned for this dataset and would need recalibration on any new population before being trusted.

6 · Photo conditions

The quality pre-check is a heuristic. Hair, glare, rulers, markers and unusual lighting can still mislead the models.

Privacy

Everything runs locally. Images and results are stored in website/storage on this computer and can be deleted from the Case history page at any time. No data is sent to third parties.
