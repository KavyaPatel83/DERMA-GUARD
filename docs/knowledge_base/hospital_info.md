# HealthSphere Dermatology Centre: services and portal guide

This is a demonstration hospital used to showcase the DERMA-GUARD research system. Hours, departments and doctors are sample content.

## Departments
General Dermatology handles rashes, acne, eczema, infections and routine skin concerns. Dermatologic Oncology handles suspected and confirmed skin cancer, mole mapping and skin cancer follow-up. Dermatologic Surgery performs excisions, Mohs surgery and biopsies. Paediatric Dermatology sees children and teenagers. Cosmetic and Laser Dermatology handles scar, laser and aesthetic treatments.

## Opening hours
Outpatient clinics run Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. The urgent skin clinic accepts suspected skin cancer referrals on weekdays. For emergencies outside these hours go to the nearest emergency department.

## Booking an appointment
Patients can book from the Appointments page after signing in. Choose a doctor or department, pick one of the available 30 minute slots, and add a short reason. A case analysed by DERMA-GUARD can be attached so the doctor sees the images and evidence in advance. Appointments can be cancelled from the same page. Doctors confirm or complete appointments from their dashboard.

## Requesting a doctor review
After an AI analysis the patient can send the case to a dermatologist for review. The doctor sees the photograph, patient details, model evidence and the assistant's summary, then records a decision and notes. The patient sees the doctor's review on the case page. Reviews are not instant, so use the appointment system or emergency services for anything urgent.

## Privacy and data
Cases are visible only to the patient who created them and the doctor they are assigned to. Administrators manage accounts and see system statistics but not clinical content. Every sensitive action is written to an audit log. Patients can delete their own cases at any time.

## Accounts and roles
Patients register themselves with an email and a password of at least eight characters including a letter and a digit. Doctor and administrator accounts are created by an administrator. Accounts lock for ten minutes after five failed sign-in attempts.

## Contact
For billing, records or general enquiries, use the front desk. This portal does not provide emergency services.
